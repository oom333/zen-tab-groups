Tab Groups

Keep your favorite sites at the top of the sidebar. Each one gets its own section with a colored header, and new tabs jump straight into place.

Your sections

Shown at the top of your tabs, in this order. Drag ⠿ to reorder. A website can only be in one section. With several spaces, pick which ones a section is used in.

    No sections yet. Add a website or a collection below.

    Website

    One site with its own section, e.g. YouTube. Named and colored from the site's icon automatically.

    Collection

    Several sites under a name and color you choose, e.g. AI for ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini.

    Collection ideas

    Starting points you can add with one click and edit afterwards. Sites already in another section are skipped.

    Appearance

    Background Fill the header, the section's tabs, or both with a light shade of its color.
    Collapse arrow Where the arrow appears when you hover a header. Click the header to fold its tabs away, with or without the arrow.
    Tab count How many tabs a section has, next to its name.

    Behavior

    Group from How many of a section's tabs must be open in a space before they're grouped. With fewer, they stay where they are.
    Header button Shown when you hover a header. Clear closes the section's tabs; Unload keeps them but frees their memory, like a folder's button.

    Backup

    Save your sections and options to a file, to keep them safe or move them to another computer. Restoring a backup replaces your current sections and options.

    Preview

    Changes save instantly and show up in your sidebar right away.
